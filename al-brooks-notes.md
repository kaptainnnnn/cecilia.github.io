## Channels

Every market at all times is in many channels, including bull and bear channels.
A broader channel may consist of many tight channels. For example, a broad bull channel contains a tight bear channel. 

Two lines form a channel.
Overshoot: part of bars going beyond line
Undershoot: distance between bars not reaching the line and the line
Channels are not always perfect. There are overshoots and undershoots sometimes.
